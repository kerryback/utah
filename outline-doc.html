About the speaker
Kerry Back teaches asset pricing theory to PhD students in the Jones School and the Department of Economics at Rice, data-driven finance in the Master of Data Science program, and generative AI for finance and data management to MBA students. He also teaches a monthly online executive education course on agentic AI. Before Rice, he was on the faculties of Northwestern, Indiana, Washington University in St. Louis — where he served as Senior Associate Dean at the Olin School — and Texas A&M. He is a former editor of the Review of Financial Studies and of Finance & Stochastics, a former associate editor of the Journal of Finance, and the author of two textbooks. He received the Financial Management Association’s Innovation in Teaching Award in 2023 and 2025, and the Jones School’s PhD Mentoring Award in 2024 and 2026.
